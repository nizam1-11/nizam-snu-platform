## 2. ABOUT INTRO

For creators who publish the same style of video each week, the small repeated steps are what take the time: fetching clips, trimming them, adding text or captions, uploading to each Page. This Windows app puts those steps into one repeatable pipeline. You connect your own accounts, choose your edits, and let a queued run carry the work from download to post. Safety limits stay in place, so the rhythm remains yours.

## 4. HOW IT WORKS

1. **Connect a source.** Paste a channel or profile link, or start from a template, a shared Google Sheet or a folder, and pick the videos.
2. **Choose your edits.** Adjust effects yourself or apply a preset — captions, colour, sound, logo and output size.
3. **Let it run safely.** Up to four videos move from download to edit to save at a time; each one finishes completely before the next group starts. Finished videos move into a paced posting queue for your Pages.

## 5. BUILT FOR SAFETY

Posting keeps a human rhythm. The app works through your own connected accounts and follows platform safety limits. Daily caps, minimum spacing, jitter, posting-hour windows, duplicate protection and dry-run validation all run by default. If a Page's health looks off, posting auto-pauses. A manual Run now can opt into a safety bypass; scheduled posts and the pause, token, duplicate and content checks are never bypassed.

## 6. FAQ

**Is it Windows only?**
Yes. It is a Windows 10/11 desktop app. There is no web or mobile version.

**Do I need a licence key?**
Yes. It uses a machine-bound licence key with configurable seats. If you change PCs, you can reactivate from the operator dashboard.

**Does it bypass platform rules?**
No. It works through your own connected accounts and follows platform safety limits — daily caps, spacing, posting windows and duplicate checks stay on.

**Can I use my own logo, captions and music on another PC?**
Yes. Settings Export/Import packs your logo, subtitle and music files with the settings, so you can move them to another PC.

**What if a download fails?**
The app retries automatically with a visible countdown. Errors are plain messages — an expired session, a private video, or a cookie refresh being needed — and there is a YouTube cookie/session refresh helper. You can cancel a batch at any time.

**Will I see a SmartScreen warning when installing?**
Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer is not code-signed yet. A signed build is planned when a code-signing certificate is available.
